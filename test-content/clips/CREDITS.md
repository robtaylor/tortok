Each clip is a 20-second excerpt, center-cropped to 9:16 portrait, and
re-encoded to H.264 + AAC. Generated by `../make-test-clips.sh` from the
1080p H.264 master on `download.blender.org`.

These clips exist solely as dev fixtures for the WebTorrent seeding
